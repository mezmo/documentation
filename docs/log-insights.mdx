---
title: "Log Insights"
description: "Review how your log patterns change over time, compare them with an earlier period, and share a link to the view you're looking at."
---

Log Insights groups your account's log lines into patterns and shows how those patterns change over a time range. Use it to see what changed in your logs compared with an earlier period, and to share that view with your team.

Log Insights shows data once Mezmo has processed your account's log lines into patterns. If you have just started sending logs, check back in a few minutes.

## Open Log Insights

Open Log Insights in either of these ways:

- In the left navigation, open **Live Tail**, then select **Log Insights**.
- On the Home page, select the **Log Insights** quick link.

Log Insights opens on the **Overview** tab. Its tabs are:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Overview** | The account timeline and a summary of what changed across your logs. |
| **Patterns** | The log patterns in the selected period, which you can filter and search by pattern text. |
| **Apps, hosts, nodes** | How the apps, hosts, and nodes sending your logs changed. |

## Choose a Time Range and Comparison

The time range and comparison you choose apply to every tab.

1. At the top of the page, select a time range: **1h**, **6h**, **24h**, or **3d**. The default is **24h**.
2. In the **compared with** list, choose the period to compare against:
   - **the period before**: the same length of time immediately before the selected range
   - **the same time yesterday**
   - **the same day last week**

## Share or Bookmark a View

The page URL keeps the tab you're on, the time range, and the comparison. Reload the page or open a shared link and Log Insights opens on the same view.

To share a view, copy the URL from your browser's address bar. For example, this URL opens the **Patterns** tab for the last 6 hours compared with the same time yesterday:

```text
https://app.mezmo.com/<account-id>/log-insights/patterns?range=6h&compare=yesterday
```

The URL uses these query parameters:

| Parameter | Values |
| --- | --- |
| `range` | `1h`, `6h`, `24h`, or `3d` |
| `compare` | `prev` (the period before), `yesterday` (the same time yesterday), or `lastweek` (the same day last week) |

If a link leaves out a parameter or gives a value that isn't listed, Log Insights uses your current selection, which starts at **24h** compared with the period before.

The URL doesn't keep a focused slice of the timeline or an app filter, so a shared link opens on the whole time range for all apps.

Changing the time range or comparison updates the URL without adding an entry to your browser history. Selecting **Back** in your browser skips your earlier time range and comparison selections.
