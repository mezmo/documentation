Mezmo no longer offers a self-service free trial. If you are new to Mezmo, [contact our team](https://www.mezmo.com/contact) to discuss your use case, access, and available plans. If you already have access, [sign in to the Mezmo app](https://app.mezmo.com).

The components and usage available to your organization depend on its plan and configuration. See [Sources](/telemetry-pipelines/set-up-pipeline-sources), [Destinations](/telemetry-pipelines/set-up-pipeline-destinations), and [Processors](/telemetry-pipelines/set-up-pipeline-processors) to learn how to build Pipelines once you have access.

## Existing Organizations

If you already belong to an organization, your administrator can confirm its current plan and access. For changes to your plan or help with an existing trial-era account, [contact our team](https://www.mezmo.com/contact).
