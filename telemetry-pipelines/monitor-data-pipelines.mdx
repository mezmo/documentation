On your Pipelines dashboard, you can monitor the volume of data flowing through your Pipelines. The dashboard shows aggregated Ingress and Egress for all Pipelines, your busiest Sources and Destinations, and volume metrics for each Pipeline, Source, and Destination.

## Access the Monitoring Dashboard
1. Log in to the [Mezmo Web App](https://app.mezmo.com/).
2. In the left-hand navigation, click the **Pipelines** icon.
3. Click **Dashboard**.
4. Select a time period from the time picker in the upper-right corner of the dashboard. All metrics on the dashboard adjust to the selected time period.

## Summary Tiles
The tiles at the top of the dashboard summarize the selected time period:

- **Total Ingress**: Data received by all of your Pipelines.
- **Total Egress**: Data sent to external Destinations.
- **Total Internal**: Data routed internally within Mezmo.
- **Pipelines**: The total number of Pipelines, and how many are running, paused, or in draft.

When data for the previous period is available, the Ingress and Egress tiles show the change compared to the previous period.

## Ingestion and Egress by Volume
The **Ingestion & egress by volume** chart shows the total ingress and egress for all of your Pipelines over the selected time period. Use it to spot spikes or drops in traffic.

- Hover over the chart to view the metrics for a specific point in time.
- Drag across the chart to zoom in on a shorter time range. The rest of the dashboard updates to match. Click **Reset zoom** to return to your previous time period.
- Use the chart type switch to display the data as a line chart or a bar chart.
- Click a series in the legend to hide or show it.

## Aggregated Egress
Next to the volume chart, the **Aggregated Egress** chart shows how egress is divided among your top three Destinations by volume, including internal routing. Volume for all other Destinations is grouped as **Other**.

## Top Components
The **Top components** card lists your top 10 Sources and top 10 Destinations by volume for the selected time period. Each entry shows the Pipeline it belongs to and its volume. Click an entry to open it in its Pipeline.

## All Pipelines
The **All Pipelines** table lists your Pipelines with their status, number of Sources and Destinations, and Ingress, Egress, and internal volume. Click a column heading, such as **Ingress** or **Egress**, to sort the table by that column.

Click the name of any Pipeline to monitor it. The Pipeline view shows its status, the version that is deployed, an **Ingestion & egress by volume** chart for that Pipeline, and the Pipeline graph with live throughput for each component. Click a node in the graph to inspect it, or click **Show a chart per node** to view volume charts for each Source and Destination. Click **Edit Pipeline** if you want to make any changes to the architecture. After you edit the Pipeline and deploy the changes, click **Monitor deployed pipeline** to view the impact of your changes.

## Sources
Click **Sources** under **Dashboard** to view a list of all Sources, the names of their associated Pipelines, their Ingress volume, and a trend line for each. Click a Source to view it in the context of the Pipeline graph. The page also shows a **Total Ingress** chart and tiles for the total number of Sources and their aggregated Ingress.

## Destinations
Click **Destinations** under **Dashboard** to view a list of all Destinations, the names of their associated Pipelines, their Egress volume, and a trend line for each. Click a Destination to view it in the context of the Pipeline graph. The page also shows a **Total Egress** chart and tiles for the total number of Destinations and their aggregated Egress.
