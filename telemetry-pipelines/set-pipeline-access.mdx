---
title: "Set Pipeline Access"
description: "Grant organization users access to the pipelines you create by assigning them the Member + Pipeline role."
---

You can provide other users of your organization with access to Pipelines that you create. 

1. Follow [the instructions to add a user](/docs/manage-members) to your organization.
2. When the new user accepts the invitation to join your organization, set the **Role** for that user to **Member + Pipeline.**
